I'm a software developer who enjoys building small, useful tools from scratch, and understanding every piece along the way.

Most of what I make is **local-first** and dependency-light: apps that run on your device with no accounts or servers, and code I can read end to end. I move between languages depending on the problem: **Kotlin** for multiplatform apps, **Go** for desktop utilities, **TypeScript** for the web and **GDScript** when I'm making games in Godot.

Right now I'm working on:

- **MUSA**, an inventory and sales app for digital artists who also sell physical stock (Kotlin Multiplatform: Android, desktop and iOS).
- **GrimWorld**, a game made with Godot.
- This site, where I write about what I build and what I learn.

Outside of code I like worldbuilding and fantasy, which is why tools like **PMythMaker** and small games like **Grimory** or **Archmage Tower** keep showing up in my repos.
